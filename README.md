# English

Cannon brings the look of the Android launcher (LineageOS Trebuchet) to iOS. First piece: a Trebuchet-style swipe-up app drawer. The App Library stays where it is; the drawer is added alongside it.

## Status

Early release (v0.0.1), not fully tested on a device yet. To try the first build, check the **Actions** tab.

## Usage

- Swipe up on the home screen to see all your apps.
- Swipe down (when the list is at the top) or tap the dimmed background: the drawer closes and you are back on your home page.
- Do not start the swipe from the very bottom edge of the screen; that edge belongs to Control Center.

## Values taken from Trebuchet

Referenced from the LineageOS Trebuchet `lineage-20.0` source (Apache 2.0): 4 columns, icon size 60, label size 14, cell height 104, cell spacing 16, a 48-high pill-shaped search field.

## Requirements

- A rootless jailbreak (palera1n rootless or Dopamine)
- An arm64 device (A8-A11, for example iPhone 7). A12 and newer (arm64e) devices are not supported right now.
- iOS 15 is the target. iOS 16 and newer have not been fully tested yet.
